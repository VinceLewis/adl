# AC-0002: Separators canonicalise to one storage separator

Whitespace and punctuation are not retained as independent storage-name
syntax; adjacent separators collapse into the one canonical underscore. This
negative boundary prevents arbitrary separator spelling from leaking into
storage metadata.
